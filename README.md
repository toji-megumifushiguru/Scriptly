🎬 Scriptly – AI-Powered Screenplay Generation Platform

Turn your ideas into professional screenplays.

Scriptly is a Generative AI-powered web application designed to transform short, unstructured story ideas into structured and editable screenplay drafts. It assists writers in developing story concepts, characters, plot structures, and screenplay scenes through an AI-driven workflow.

🌟 About the Project

Writing a screenplay often requires creativity, planning, character development, and a structured narrative. For aspiring writers, converting a simple idea into a properly organized screenplay can be challenging.

Scriptly simplifies this process by leveraging Generative AI to assist users in transforming their ideas into structured stories and screenplay drafts.

Users can provide a story idea, select a genre and tone, and generate screenplay-related content, including story details, character profiles, act structures, and scenes.

The platform is designed for aspiring screenwriters, filmmakers, content creators, students, and storytelling enthusiasts.

✨ Features

🤖 AI-Powered Screenplay Generation – Transform story ideas into screenplay drafts.

📖 Story Development – Generate story titles, taglines, loglines, and synopses.

👥 Character Development – Generate character profiles with roles and descriptions.

🎭 Genre Selection – Customize screenplay generation based on genres such as Thriller, Romance, Fantasy, Mystery, Drama, and Comedy.

🎨 Tone Selection – Guide the storytelling style and emotional direction.

🎬 Three-Act Structure – Organize stories into structured narrative acts.

📝 Screenplay Scenes – Generate scene-based screenplay content.

✏️ Screenplay Editing – Review and edit generated screenplay content.

📂 Project Dashboard – View and manage screenplay projects.

🔐 Authentication – User registration and login functionality.

📄 PDF Export – Export screenplay content as PDF.
